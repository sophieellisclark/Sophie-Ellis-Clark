Real Books. Different Audiences. One Focus: Discovery.

Every book has its own audience, story, positioning and discovery opportunities. Effective marketing begins with understanding those differences.

Dobi Daniels
Loving the Billionaire Army Doc
Sweet Contemporary Billionaire Romance | Dexington Doctor Billionaires Series
The Book

Loving the Billionaire Army Doc is a sweet contemporary romance that blends billionaire romance with a medical setting, enemies-to-lovers tension, and an arranged marriage premise. The story follows Dr. Jasmine Banks and billionaire reconstructive surgeon Dr. David Landi, former romantic partners who are pushed toward an arranged-marriage dating situation.

The book combines emotional conflict, forced proximity, second-chance romance, and a strong female protagonist, giving it multiple layers of appeal for romance readers.

The Discovery Opportunity

This type of romance has several reader interest points that create strong discovery opportunities:

  • Billionaire romance
  • Doctor romance
  • Enemies-to-lovers
  • Arranged marriage
  • Second-chance romance
  • Strong female protagonist
  • Emotional romance
  • Series readers

Each of these angles appeals to a slightly different segment of romance readers, and a strategic approach can identify which communities, promotional channels, and content themes are most relevant.

The Strategic Approach

An effective discovery strategy for a book like this begins by identifying the reader communities and promotional angles that align with the book's strongest hooks. This might include romance reader groups, contemporary romance blogs, and content that speaks directly to the tropes and emotional beats that these readers actively search for.

The goal is not to tell everyone about the book, but to make it visible to the readers who are already looking for this kind of story.

The Reader Connection

When the book's strongest reader hooks are understood, promotional messaging becomes more relevant and less generic. Instead of trying to appeal to all romance readers, the focus shifts to connecting with the specific readers who will genuinely love this story.

Kate Cullen
Game On Boys! The PlayStation Play-offs
Children's Action & Adventure | Game On Boys Series
The Book

Game On Boys! The PlayStation Play-offs is a children's action and adventure chapter book. The story follows 10-year-old Ryan James and his adventures surrounding a PlayStation competition at school, combining gaming, humor, school adventures, friendship, and relatable children's experiences.

The book has strong reluctant-reader appeal, making it a valuable addition to classrooms, libraries, and family bookshelves.

The Discovery Opportunity

This book can appeal to several connected audiences, which creates multiple discovery pathways:

  • Children who love gaming
  • Reluctant readers
  • Parents
  • Teachers
  • Educators
  • Children interested in school adventures
  • Families looking for humorous chapter books
  • Young readers who enjoy relatable characters

Each of these audiences may discover the book through different channels, from online parenting communities to educator networks and library recommendations.

The Strategic Approach

Children's books often require thinking beyond the child reader. A discovery strategy for this book considers all of the people involved in a child's reading experience:

  • Child reader
  • Parent
  • Teacher
  • School
  • Library
  • Reading group
  • Gift buyer

Understanding all of these audiences creates more opportunities for discovery, because each one may encounter the book in a different context.

The Reader Connection

The book's gaming theme, humor, school setting, and accessible adventure create multiple content and outreach angles. These can be used to connect with the specific communities who are most likely to champion the book.
