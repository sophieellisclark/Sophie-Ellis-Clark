Real Books. Different Audiences. One Focus: Discovery.

Every book has its own audience, story, positioning and discovery opportunities. Effective marketing begins with understanding those differences.

Results Are Built on Strategy, Not Luck

I work with authors across genres to improve discoverability and reader engagement. My approach is tailored to each book's unique positioning, audience, and goals. I do not rely on generic tactics or guaranteed outcomes; instead, I build strategies that make your book visible to the readers most likely to love it.

Whether your book is a romance, a children's adventure, a memoir, or a thriller, the principles of discovery remain the same: understand the book, identify the readers, and find the channels where they gather.

Reader-Centered

I focus on connecting your book with the readers who are already looking for what you wrote.

Genre-Aware

Each genre has its own communities, tropes, and discovery channels. I leverage these to your advantage.

Strategic & Transparent

My process is clear and measurable. You'll know exactly what I'm doing and why.

Author Privacy & Reference Policy

Your trust matters to me. So does the privacy of the authors I work with.

I respect and protect the privacy of every author I work with. I do not publicly publish private author contact information, personal correspondence, or identifiable references simply for the purpose of proving my work.

Protecting this information also helps reduce the risk of unsolicited outreach, impersonation, fake requests, misuse of an author's name, and other forms of unwanted contact.

I believe professional results should be presented responsibly, without compromising the privacy of the people behind those results.

Want to verify a result or request a reference?

I completely understand that you may want additional reassurance before working with me. If you would like to verify a particular result, collaboration, or reference, please contact me directly.

Where appropriate, I can explain the available verification process and, with the necessary permission, facilitate an introduction to a relevant reference.

Please do not attempt to locate or contact authors independently using private information. Contact me first, and I will handle any appropriate introduction respectfully.

Interested in verifying my work?

Contact me directly and I'll be happy to discuss the appropriate verification process.
