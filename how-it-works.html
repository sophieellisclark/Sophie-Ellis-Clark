How It Works

A clear, transparent process for book discovery and marketing.

01

Book Review

We start with a comprehensive review of your book's current market presence. This includes assessing your book page, positioning, reader engagement, and existing promotional efforts.

  • Assessment of current visibility
  • Review of your book page and metadata
  • Evaluation of your author platform
02

Discovery Strategy

Based on the review, we build a tailored discovery strategy that addresses your book's specific strengths, weaknesses, and opportunities.

  • Ideal reader definition
  • Positioning refinement
  • Channel prioritization
03

Campaign Planning

We develop a detailed campaign plan that maps out promotional activities, timelines, and resources. You'll know exactly what will happen and when.

  • Promotional calendar
  • Outreach list
  • Content plan
04

Promotion

We execute the plan across channels, engaging reader communities, book clubs, and content platforms to build visibility for your book.

  • Reader community outreach
  • Content distribution
  • Campaign execution
05

Reporting & Refinement

We track performance and provide transparent reporting. This allows us to refine the strategy based on real data, not guesswork.

  • Performance tracking
  • Insight reports
  • Strategy adjustments
