About Sophie Ellis Clark

Book Discovery & Marketing Strategist

A Strategic Partner for Your Book's Visibility

Sophie Ellis Clark is a Book Discovery & Marketing Strategist who helps authors navigate the complex world of book marketing and discoverability. Her work focuses on one central question: how do we make sure the right readers find your book?

With a deep understanding of the publishing landscape, reader behavior, and digital marketing, Sophie builds tailored strategies that help authors move beyond guesswork and into a clear, actionable plan for visibility.

Sophie's approach is grounded in education, strategy, and genuine care for authors and their work. She believes that every book deserves a chance to be discovered by the readers who will love it, and she works tirelessly to make that happen.

What Sophie Believes

Strategy First

Marketing should never be random. Every action should serve a purpose and align with a clear strategy.

Education Over Hype

Authors deserve to understand the why behind the strategy, not just the what. Education empowers long-term success.

Readers Are People

Marketing is not manipulation. It is about connecting the right book with the right reader in an authentic way.
